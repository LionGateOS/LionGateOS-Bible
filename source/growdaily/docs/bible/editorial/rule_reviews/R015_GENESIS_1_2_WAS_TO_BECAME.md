# R015 — Genesis 1:2 "was" → "became"

**Rule ID:** R015
**Family:** Genesis 1:2 translation correction
**Status:** EVIDENCE_AUDIT
**Recorded:** 2026-10-16 (EDT)

---

## 1. Transformation Description

**R015** transforms the word "was" to "became" in Genesis 1:2.

Genesis 1:2 (KJV): "And the earth **was** without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep."

Genesis 1:2 (GD): "And the earth **became** without form and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep."

This is a translation correction based on the Hebrew temporal sense of the verb, indicating a change of state rather than an initial condition.
